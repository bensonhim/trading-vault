## Context: The Performance Cycle

> "My accounts have been sideways for the past two months or so. The market hasn't really been great for my style of trading, my momentum and swing trading... I had a big big run from about mid September until about mid March. Then April, May, June were very small up months. July was a red month for me, first red month in probably a year and August so far I'm red also."

**Cycle:** Big run (Sep–Mar) → small up months (Apr–Jun) → red months (Jul–Aug). Style mismatch with the market regime.

## Tesla Short

> "Tesla I've been short for a few days. I added more today near the open. I've been short since here since this day here when it looked like it was going to break down from the sixty minute range after the bogus Elon Musk tweets where he said he's gonna take the company private. I don't believe that one bit."

> "I am overall bearish on Tesla, the fundamentals and everything. I think the company has too many problems right now, but the stock is down quite a bit from the highs. It could very easily snap back just as a technical bounce, but I think this thing is starting to deteriorate fundamentally."

**Thesis:** Fundamental deterioration + bogus take-private tweet = short setup. Risk: technical snap-back bounce.

## The Software Group Theme

> "This is one of the strongest groups right now, this small mid cap software stocks."

He's long two software names ([[Sent]], [[SALE]]) — both recent IPOs, both with earnings, both in the strongest current group. **Group strength is a setup qualifier.**

## Earnings Winner Mechanics (Recurring Pattern)

Multiple positions are **earnings winners bought on the post-earnings reaction**:
- RH — earnings, pullback to 50-day, bounce
- ETSY — gapped up, sold off, bought the fade
- SODA — blowout, opening range highs
- SALE — earnings days ago, higher lows all year

The common thread: **earnings is the catalyst, the entry is on the technical setup after the earnings reaction** (gap-up fade, 50-day bounce, opening range highs).

## Risk Discipline — Two Stops per Position

> "I have two stops for it. This is the area where I'll sell half and here I'll sell the other half in case it stops me out."

**Scaling out, not all-or-nothing:** sell half at first level, full stop at second. This is his standard position management.

## Lessons

1. **Style cycles matter.** Momentum swing trading has regime mismatches — 8 months of chop after a 6-month run is normal, not a system failure.
2. **Short squeeze thesis is a valid long rationale** — high short interest + hate + a price trigger can squeeze regardless of fundamentals.
3. **Group strength** (software small/mid-caps here) is a setup qualifier — trade the strongest group.
4. **Two stops per position** (half out + full stop) is his scaling discipline.
5. **Earnings winners** are bought on the technical reaction, not the earnings event itself.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Be transparent about performance cycles** — red months happen even after big runs; the edge is the style, not the month.
2. **Short squeeze setups** (high short interest + hated name + trigger) are tradeable longs regardless of fundamentals.
3. **Software small/mid-caps** were the strongest group in Aug 2018 — trade the strongest group.
4. **Position management** = two stops (sell-half + full-stop), not binary.
5. **Earnings entries** are technical (gap fade, 50-day bounce, ORH), not event-driven.
