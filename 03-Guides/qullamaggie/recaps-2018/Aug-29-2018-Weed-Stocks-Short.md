# August 29 — Weed Stocks Short Squeeze & TLRY Fade

Kristjan's main thesis: weed stocks (TLRY, CGC, CRON) are in a **parabolic mania** trading at 200× sales — he can't bring himself to buy them long despite textbook setups, so he's shorting the fade and trading the squeeze mechanics, capped by a painful $40k two-minute profit giveback.

---

## The Weed Stock Mania Thesis

> "This thing trades 200 times sales. Like what can it say on the earnings to make it go higher after the big run it's already had? Like, 200 times sales. It's just insane valuation on it, but it doesn't matter. Everyone is short it and they covered it."

> "It's all hype and mania and excitement... it's a great story to sell, but I don't think this move is sustainable."

**Analogs cited:**
- **GoPro** — traded ~20× sales post-IPO, gave back everything, -30% in a few days
- **Fitbit** — ~20× sales, same pattern

> "These things, it's all hype and mania and excitement in new... it's never been legal before, and now a lot of countries starting to legalize it. Then a lot of new products coming out with Mariana in it. It's just exciting and new, and it's a great story to sell, but I don't think this move is sustainable."

## The Mental Block — Can't Buy Parabolic Longs

> "I just can't buy these things. It's very hard for me to buy these things. Like, here, for example, there was a great long setup in it after a few days of consolidation, but I just couldn't buy it. It's impossible."

> "I probably would be much richer if I got rid of this mental block, but whatever. It is what it is. I'm just focusing on shorting these things. It works for me. Whatever. Can't trade them perfectly, both long and short."

**Key self-awareness:** He recognizes the long setups are there, acknowledges the mental block costs him money, but has decided to play to his strength (shorting) rather than fight it.

## The $40k Two-Minute Profit Giveback

> "I covered it literally here and here when it started taking out these intraday ranges. So it went lower initially, and on this spike, I kinda gave back, like, 40,000 in profits in two minutes. That was probably my quickest loss ever, or in this case, I gave back profits. So that was a record for me."

**Lesson:** Covering a short into a spike (when it takes out intraday ranges to the upside) is correct risk management — the giveback is the cost of the discipline. Don't hold through a squeeze hoping it fades again.

## Shorting the Parabolic — The Plan for TLRY

> "I hope this thing goes to 70 today and maybe 80 tomorrow. That would be really great, but I will take any short setup. If it starts showing weakness, I will take a starter short and start scaling in as it takes out ranges to the downside because I think this thing has a big fade in it eventually."

**Mechanics:**
1. Hope for more upside (build the parabolic)
2. Wait for **weakness** — don't short into strength
3. **Starter short** on first weakness
4. **Scale in** as it takes out ranges to the downside

This is the [[Trading Meeting Knowledge|parabolic short]] setup — don't pick the top, wait for the rollover.

## The AMD Walk-Away Lesson

> "I was tired and I had taken a bunch of losses the whole day. I wanted to clear my head and I went for a walk and then it tanked quite a bit, so that was annoying."

**Lesson:** Loss fatigue is real — taking a walk after a losing streak caused him to miss the payoff fade. The discipline of stepping away has a cost.

## Relative Strength Filtering for Shorts

> "Intraday, it started showing relative strength to the other weed names. And this one, I probably won't look at on the short side at all. I'm gonna focus on TLRY and GTC on the next few days on any good setups."

**Rule:** Within a parabolic group, **short the weakest, not the strongest.** CRON showed RS → don't short it. TLRY/CGC = the short candidates.

## Scaling Out of Long Swings

> "I've been scaling out of my long swings like sold some Roku and Etsy and RH and I've been selling a few shares pretty much daily."

**Daily selling into strength** on long-term winners — consistent with his two-stops discipline.

## Lessons

1. **Parabolic manias are shortable, not buyable** (for Kristjan) — 200× sales is the valuation signal, GoPro/Fitbit are the analogs.
2. **Mental blocks are expensive but honest.** He acknowledges the long setups are there, but plays to his short strength rather than fighting the block.
3. **Cover shorts into spikes** — the $40k two-minute giveback is the cost of discipline, not a mistake.
4. **Short the weakest in a parabolic group**, not the strongest (RS filter).
5. **Loss fatigue costs trades** — the AMD walk-away missed the fade.
6. **Starter short + scale on rollover** is the parabolic short entry mechanic.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Valuation-short thesis** (200× sales + GoPro/Fitbit analogs) identifies the parabolic; **technical rollover** times the entry.
2. **Play to your strength** — Kristjan can't buy parabolic longs; he shorts the fade instead. Honest self-assessment > fighting your wiring.
3. **Cover into spikes, scale into rollovers** — parabolic short discipline.
4. **Relative strength filters shorts**: weakest = short candidate, strongest = avoid.
5. **Profit giveback on covering is correct discipline** — the $40k two-minute giveback was right, not wrong.
