## Sector Rotation Concept

- **Classic rotation day**: Leaders (Nasdaq/tech/growth) weaken while laggards (airlines, cruise lines, oil, REITs) rally
- **Not the method Kristjan teaches** — these beaten-down sectors are "variations" of the setup, not the core breakout
- **Hot sectors** = multiple stocks making big moves simultaneously (COVID, crypto, solar, China)
- **Single stock running in a dead sector** = sketchy, avoid

> "When you have a whole sector showing strength at the same time, you just buy the strongest one. It's tricky when only one stock is strong and the others are sluggish."

## The Stair-Step Model (Reinforced)

> "Stocks move like stairs. You get a leg higher, sideways, leg higher, sideways. We are trying to identify the areas when the next step higher could be formed."

- **Tight areas** = tight stop = good risk/reward
- **Wide action before breakout** = bad — you need tightness
- **This is how stocks have moved for 100 years** — and will continue to

## Entry Rules (Refined)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Stop ≤ 1× ATR** | If the stop (lows of day) is more than 1 ATR away, don't buy |
| **Ideal entry** | Stock up 1/3 to 2/3 of ATR on the day |
| **Too late** | Stock already up more than full ATR — chasing |
| **Opening range highs** | 1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle breakout |
| **Earnings on chart** | Add earnings dates to TC2000 — never buy within 3 days of earnings |

## Lessons Learned

1. **Sector rotation is real but not the core method** — airlines/cruise lines rallying while tech sells off is a variation, not the primary setup.
2. **Hot sector context upgrades setups** — a 3.5-star setup in a hot sector (COVID) counts as a 5-star because of increased success probability.
3. **Run three scans daily** — 1-month, 3-month, 6-month gainers. After market close, find setups, add to watchlist.
4. **Tightness before breakout is mandatory** — wide action = bad setup, even if it works in hindsight.
5. **Earnings dates on charts** — add them in TC2000. Never buy within 3 days of earnings.
6. **Insider buys/sells are irrelevant** — trade price, not fundamentals. MRNA had massive insider sales and still went up 200%.
