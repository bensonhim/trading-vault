## Key Concepts

### The 45-Degree Angle (Deep Dive)

The 10-day and 20-day moving averages must rise at **≥ 45 degrees**. This is the visual filter for momentum leaders.

- **45°** = minimum acceptable
- **> 45°** = strong, tradeable
- **~ 90°** = parabolic (rare, best setups)

> "You want stocks where the 10 and 20-day moving averages are in a 45-degree angle or even more. The faster, the better."

If the angle is shallow or flat, the stock has lost short-term momentum — avoid it.

### Pattern Repetition Across Decades

> "This is how stocks have moved in the past hundred years. You can go back and look at stock charts from the twenties, thirties, forties, fifties, sixties, seventies, eighties, nineties. Same exact patterns over and over again."

The setup is **timeless**. It worked 100 years ago, it works now, it will work for the next 100 years.

### Earnings Season Risk Management

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| **No profit cushion** | Don't hold through earnings — sell before |
| **Have profit cushion** | Can hold (e.g., TTD bought at $4.43, would need 10% gap down to lose) |
| **Big position + earnings** | Size down before report unless closed very strong |
| **Stock ran big into earnings** | Expect "buy the rumor, sell the news" — gap down likely |

> "I only hold through earnings if I have a profit cushion."

## ATR vs ADR

| Metric | What it measures | Includes gaps? |
|---|---|---|
| **ADR** (Average Daily Range) | (High - Low) / Close × 100 | Yes |
| **ATR** (Average True Range) | Intraday range | No |

- Kristjan uses ATR for **stop placement** — checks if stock is already up more than its ATR on the day before buying
- **Rule**: Don't buy if stock is already up more than 1× ATR from the close
- Ideal: buy when stock is up only 1/3 to 2/3 of ATR

## The Sell Rules (Complete)

| Phase | Action |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Buy at opening range highs. Stop = lows of the day. |
| **Day 3** | Sell 1/3 |
| **Day 5** | Sell another 1/3 (or sell 1/2 on day 3–5) |
| **After** | Trail remaining 1/3 to 1/2 with 10-day MA |
| **Exit** | First close below the 10-day MA = sell the rest |

> "Stop is always lows of the entry day."

> "You don't need any predictions. It doesn't matter what the Fed says, what China does, what Trump says. Only thing you need to care about is the 10-day moving average."

## Lessons Learned

1. **Patterns repeat across decades** — study charts from the 1920s, 1950s, 1980s. Same setups.
2. **45-degree angle is a visual shortcut** — if the MA slope isn't steep, the stock isn't a leader.
3. **Earnings risk is real** — don't hold through earnings without a profit cushion.
4. **ATR for entries, ADR for scans** — use ATR to judge if you're chasing, ADR to filter scan results.
5. **One setup is enough** — master the breakout method. Don't confuse yourself with shorts, EPs, or other methods until you've made your first million.
6. **Study is mandatory** — "hundreds of hours, thousands of hours over the next few years." There is no shortcut.
