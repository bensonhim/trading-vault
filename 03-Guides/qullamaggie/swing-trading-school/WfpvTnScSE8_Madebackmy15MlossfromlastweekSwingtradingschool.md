# Recovering from a $1.5M Loss — Bull Markets Forgive Mistakes

Kristjan made back a $1.5M Kodak loss in under a week by sticking to the breakout method. This session covers market regime identification, the parabolic short setup, and why holding winners is the hardest skill.

---

## The $1.5M Loss & Recovery

- **July 29**: Accidental Kodak short → $1.5M loss (8% of account)
- **Recovery in 5 days**: W ($500k), CODX ($500k), CHWY + ZM + BEALY + TTD (combined)
- **Lesson**: A bull market forgives mistakes. In a good market you can have many fuck-ups and still make it back.

> "That's what a bull market does. It's very forgiving to mistakes."

## Market Regime Identification

Kristjan uses the **Nasdaq 10-day and 20-day moving averages** as his market filter:

| Regime | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Momentum market** | 10-day rising, 20-day rising, 10 > 20 | Trade aggressively — double/triple account |
| **Choppy/sideways** | MAs flattish, no clear trend | Break even at best; minimal trading |
| **Downtrend** | 10-day sloping down, 20-day sloping down, 10 < 20 | Sit out. No breakout trading. |

> "You don't need to trade all the time. It's fine to sit in cash for months on end."

- 90% of stocks Kristjan trades are Nasdaq stocks. S&P 500 and NYSE are irrelevant to him.
- Every year has 3–6 month good periods and 3–6 month bad periods. Wait them out.

## The Three Setups

Kristjan only trades **three setups**:

1. **Breakouts** (the one he teaches) — big move, consolidation, tight range, breakout
2. **Episodic Pivot** — earnings/news catalyst gap (not covered in detail here)
3. **Parabolic Short** — extended stock, 3–5 days up, gets parabolic, slams down

> "Just focus on the basic breakout method. You don't need anything else to make millions."

## The Parabolic Short Setup

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Conditions** | Stock up 3–5 days in a row, extremely extended from 10/20-day MA |
| **Trigger** | Big candle, parabolic extension, can't pull back |
| **Entry** | First red 1-min candle, or opening range lows, or VWAP rejection |
| **Stop** | High of the day or recent swing high |
| **Target** | Pullback to rising 10-day or 20-day MA (often 20–30% lower) |

- **Livongo (LVGO)**: Shorted 10,000 shares at $140s, slammed to $115
- **Fiverr (FVRR)**: Extended 4–5 days, setting up as short
- **Etsy (ETSY)**: 6 days up, potential 5-star short if it gaps up on earnings

> "You can't be too early. That's the main thing on shorts."

## Sell Rules (Reinforced)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Buy at opening range highs. Stop = lows of the day. |
| **Day 3–5** | Sell 1/3 to 1/2. Move stop to breakeven. |
| **After Day 5** | Trail remaining with 10-day MA. Exit on first close below 10-day. |

> "All you have to do is wait for the first close below the 10-day moving average. And it's just so hard."

## Lessons Learned

1. **Bull markets forgive** — stick to the method, losses recover if the regime is right.
2. **Market regime is king** — check Nasdaq 10/20-day before trading breakouts.
3. **Holding winners is the hardest skill** — Kristjan sold LVGO, CHWY, MRNA too early. The 10-day trailing rule exists to prevent this.
4. **Shorting is not the opposite of going long** — different mechanics, much harder. Don't short until you've made your first million.
5. **Dan Zanger and Pradeep Bonde** are key influences — same momentum method, timeless setup.
6. **Fundamentals = fuel** — big earnings/revenue growth drives the biggest moves (LVGO, W, FSLY, FVRR, GSX).
