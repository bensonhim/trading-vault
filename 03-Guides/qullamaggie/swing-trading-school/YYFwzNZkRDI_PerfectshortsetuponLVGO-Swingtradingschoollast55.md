# Perfect Short on LVGO — Scans, Setups, and the 45-Degree Angle

Kristjan shorts Livongo perfectly, then walks through his scanning method, the 45-degree angle concept, and dozens of historical five-star setups. This session is the most detailed breakdown of the breakout setup anatomy.

## The Scanning Method

Kristjan uses **TC2000** with three scans:

| Scan | Formula | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **1-month gainers** | `C - MinLow(22)` ranked ≥ 90% | Recent momentum |
| **3-month gainers** | `C - MinLow(67)` ranked ≥ 90% | Medium-term momentum |
| **6-month gainers** | `C - MinLow(126)` ranked ≥ 90% | Longer-term momentum |

**Filters applied to all scans:**
- **Dollar volume** ≥ $10–80M (scale by account: account × 50)
- **ADR (20-day)** ≥ 4% (Kristjan) / ≥ 5–6% (small accounts)
- **Rank** ≥ 90–95%

> "If you get more than 50 results on any scan, tighten the criteria — increase ADR, volume, or rank."

**ADR Formula (TC2000):**
```
ADR = (High - Low) / Close × 100, averaged over 20 days
```
Kristjan posted this on his about page for users to replicate.

## The 45-Degree Angle

A critical filter: the 10-day and 20-day moving averages should be rising at **≥ 45 degrees**.

- **> 45°** = strong momentum, good setup
- **< 45°** = sluggish, avoid
- **~ 90°** = parabolic (best, but rare)

> "You want stocks that go up in 45-degree angles or even more. The faster, the better."

If the moving averages are sloping flat or down, the stock has lost momentum — avoid it.

## Setup Anatomy (The Perfect Five-Star)

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| 1. **Big move** | Stock up 100%+ over weeks/months |
| 2. **Pullback/sideways** | Finds support on rising 10, 20, or 50-day MA |
| 3. **Higher lows** | Builds progressively higher lows over multiple days |
| 4. **Tight range** | Candles get narrower and narrower |
| 5. **Breakout** | High-volume breakout from the tight range |

**Entry**: Opening range highs (1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle)
**Stop**: Lows of the day
**Sell**: 1/3 to 1/2 after 3–5 days → breakeven stop → trail with 10-day → exit on first close below 10-day

## Lessons Learned

1. **Build a pattern database** — thousands of screenshots in Evernote/folders. This is the real work.
2. **45-degree angle is a hard filter** — if MAs aren't rising steeply, the stock isn't a momentum leader.
3. **Shorting requires more experience** — don't short until you've made your first million. Ignore all short talk until then.
4. **Earnings = fuel** — triple-digit EPS and revenue growth drive the biggest moves (LVGO, W, FSLY, FVRR, GSX).
5. **Never put > 25% in one stock** — even in a bull market with hundreds of opportunities. Concentration risk is real (Elon heart attack example).
6. **Use stop market orders, not limit** — if stock tanks, limit won't fill and losses compound.
