# Crypto Setups Special — Same Patterns Across Asset Classes

Kristjan demonstrates that the exact same breakout setup he trades on stocks applies to crypto. Stocks and crypto move in **stairs** — move, sideways, move, sideways. The job is to identify the next step just as it breaks.

---

## Key Concepts

- **Stair-step movement**: All asset classes (stocks, crypto, commodities) move in stairs. Big move → consolidation → next leg. Your job: buy at the exact moment the next step begins.
- **Same patterns, any asset class**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, stocks — identical setups. Big move, pullback, surfing 10/20/50-day MA, tight range, breakout.
- **ADR (Average Daily Range)**: Use ADR ≥ 5–6% in scans. Low ADR = slow = no money. High ADR = fast = where doubles/triples happen.
- **Win rate is irrelevant**: 25% win rate is fine. Small losers, big winners. One home run makes back 50 losers.
- **Head and shoulders = shampoo**: Ignore head and shoulders patterns. 95% are failures. Focus on bull flags, high-tight flags, range breakouts.

---

## Setup Mechanics

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Entry** | Opening range highs (1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle breakout) |
| **Stop** | Lows of the day — always |
| **ATR check** | Don't buy if stock is already up more than its ATR on the day |
| **Sell rule** | Sell 1/3 to 1/2 after 3–5 days, move stop to breakeven, trail rest with 10-day MA |
| **Final exit** | First close below the 10-day moving average |
| **Position sizing** | 10–25% of account per stock; scale by liquidity and conviction |

## Crypto Walkthrough

Kristjan walks through **Bitcoin** charts from 2012 onward:
- Same stair-step pattern: move → sideways → 10/20 MA support → tight range → breakout
- One setup went from $13.50 to $2.60 — nearly a 20-bagger — without ever closing below the 10-day
- "High tide flag" identified: surfs 10/20-day, gets extremely tight, then explodes
- **Ethereum** shows same patterns; five-star setup doubled in weeks
- **Ripple / Litecoin** — fewer setups but same principle applies

## Lessons Learned

1. **Same setup works everywhere** — crypto, stocks, ETFs. Pattern recognition is transferable.
2. **Failure rate is high in choppy markets** — many good setups fail. Endure it.
3. **Small accounts have an edge** — can trade high-ADR small caps that Kristjan can't size into.
4. **Don't chase extended stocks** — wait for the base, the tightness, the breakout.
5. **Short seller hit pieces are mostly noise** — follow price, not opinions. GSX up 300% after Citron/Muddy Waters hits.
6. **Day trading is a waste of time** — swing/position trading is where big money is made.
