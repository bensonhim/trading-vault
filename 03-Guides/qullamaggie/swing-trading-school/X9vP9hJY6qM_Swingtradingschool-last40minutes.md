# Breakout Setup Deep-Dive — The One Setup That Makes Millions

Kristjan walks through his entire portfolio and dissects the breakout setup he learned from Pradeep Bonde / StockBee. This is the foundational swing trading method: big move → consolidation on moving average → tight range → breakout.

---

## The Setup (Canonical)

1. **Big move** — stock must be in the top 1–2% of strongest stocks on some timeframe (1mo, 3mo, 6mo)
2. **Pullback or sideways consolidation** — finds support on rising 10-day, 20-day, or 50-day MA
3. **Higher lows build** — chart gets tighter and tighter
4. **Tight range** — narrow candles, often a range that contracts
5. **Breakout** — takes out the range on high volume

> "If you master just this one setup, you will never have to work a day in your life."

---

## Entry Mechanics

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Entry trigger** | Opening range highs — first 1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle breakout |
| **Stop** | Lows of the entry day — always |
| **ATR check** | Don't buy if stock is already up more than ~2/3 of its ATR on the day |
| **Sell rule 1** | Sell 1/3 to 1/2 after 3–5 days |
| **Sell rule 2** | Move stop to breakeven after first sell |
| **Sell rule 3** | Trail remaining shares with 10-day MA — exit on first close below 10-day |

## Lessons Learned

1. **Memorize the setup** — take screenshots, build a database of thousands of examples.
2. **Leaders lead** — be in the strongest stocks, not laggards. Relative strength is everything.
3. **Don't sell too early** — the 10-day trailing stop catches home runs. Selling on "feeling extended" is Kristjan's biggest weakness.
4. **Pump stocks vs. institutional stocks** — both work. Pumps (CHWY, LAC, JUMIA) are retail-driven; institutional names (TDOC, AVLR, TTD, W) are fund-driven.
5. **Avoid slow stocks** — ADR < 4% is a waste of time. UPS, COST, TER — all rejected.
6. **Earnings breakouts can be powerful** — if they don't gap up 20% and pull back to reclaim highs, they can be bought.
