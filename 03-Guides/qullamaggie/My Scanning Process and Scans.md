# My Scanning Process and Scans

Kristjan walks through every TC2000 scan he uses to find setups nightly and intraday — the filters, the custom indicators, and the logic behind each one.

---

## Core Scanning Philosophy

Kristjan uses TC2000 as his primary scanning platform. He runs **nightly/weekly scans** to build watchlists and **intraday scans** to catch real-time breakouts not on his radar. His scans all share common DNA: minimum price, minimum volume, minimum dollar volume, and an **ADR (Average Daily Range) cutoff of 2.2%**.

> "I just like it to weed the slower moving stocks basically, I want stocks that have range."

### ADR vs ATR

- **ADR (Average Daily Range)** — intraday range only, excludes gaps. Cutoff: **2.2%** over 20 days.
- **ATR (Average True Range)** — includes gaps, measured in dollars.

A stock like Tesla has ADR ~2.63%, Netflix ~1.49%, Facebook ~1.06%. A microcap like HGCM showed ~25% ADR — "absolutely insanely high."

## Scan Inventory

| Scan | Purpose | Key Criteria | Frequency |
|------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| ADR stocks | Foreign ADRs sorted separately in TC2000 | Price ≥$1, Vol ≥40k, $ Vol ≥$1.5M, ADR ≥2.2% | Nightly |
| Biggest 1-month gainers | Momentum longs/shorts | Higher $ vol, higher ADR, price growth top 3% | Nightly |
| Trend Intensity | Pure high-trend scan | TI ≥108 + liquidity + ADR | Weekly |
| 5-day gainers | Best short ideas | Stocks strong past 5 days | Nightly |
| 5-day losers | Mean reversion longs | Occasional setups | Nightly |
| Biggest 1-yr / 2-yr losers | Beaten-down bounce plays | Bottom 3.45% over 1–2 years | Nightly |
| OTC scan | OTC stocks | Lower price, higher ADR, lower volume | Nightly |
| Recent IPOs | IPO momentum | Price growth 1yr = blank (under 1yr old) | Nightly |
| ETF scan | Market context | — | Occasional |

### Trend Intensity Indicator

Borrowed from **Pradeep Bonde** (stockbee.blogspot.com / stockbee.biz):

> "It basically is how much... a thirteen day moving average, how many percent that is above or below the sixty five day moving average."

- **TI = 90** → faster MA no more than 10% below slower MA
- **TI = 110** → faster MA at least 10% above slower MA
- Kristjan scans for **TI ≥108** for pure momentum

## Intraday Scans

| Scan | Trigger |
|------|---------|
| Today's momentum watchlist | Stocks up ≥55% of their ADR intraday |
| Stocks up ≥7% | News-driven breakouts across entire US market |
| ADR intraday | Which ADRs are up on the day |
| OTC intraday | What's moving on OTC |
| 1-yr / 2-yr losers intraday | Anticipate breakouts on beaten-down names |

> "These scans [intraday] are to find stocks that break out in real time or that gap up on news... stuff that's not on my watch list."

## Watchlist Management

- All nightly scans feed into **"Today's Momentum Watchlist"**
- Intraday scan filters that watchlist only
- Additional watchlists: daily tracking, sectors, random thematic lists
- TC2000 lets you flip through hundreds of charts in 20–30 minutes using down-arrow / spacebar

## Key Takeaways

1. **ADR ≥2.2% is the universal filter** — eliminates slow-moving stocks across all scans.
2. **Trend Intensity (Pradeep's indicator) ≥108** for pure momentum scanning.
3. **5-day gainers = best short ideas**; 5-day losers = occasional mean-reversion longs.
4. **1-yr / 2-yr biggest losers** have been highly profitable since the Feb market bottom — beaten-down stocks doubling/tripling from $1–2 levels.
5. **Recent IPOs** (~100 in scan) — momentum trading works especially well on them.
6. **Nightly scans build the watchlist; intraday scans catch what's not on it.**
7. TC2000's speed (spacebar navigation) is why Kristjan prefers it — hundreds of charts in 20 minutes.
