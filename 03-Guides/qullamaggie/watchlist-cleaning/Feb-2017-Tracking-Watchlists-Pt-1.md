# Feb 2017 Tracking Watchlists Cleaning — Pt 1 (Feb 1–10)

Early February 2017 was thick with breakouts — Kristjan flags multiple "five-star setups" and uses the session to demonstrate the **high-tide-flag** entry: tight range day before breakout, buy on opening range highs, stop at the candle low.

---

## Anatomy of a Five-Star Setup

Kristjan calls several charts "five star" throughout this session. The recurring criteria:

1. **Tight range candle** the day before (narrow range, consolidation)
2. **Breakout on volume** the next day
3. **Higher lows** leading into the breakout (multi-day tightness)
4. **Clean catalyst** — earnings, news, or sector theme
5. **Opening range highs** entry, candle-low stop

> "I wait for the first candle breakout then I buy it on the opening range highs or on any kind of intraday breakout."

## The NAK / Kerisdale Hit-Piece Lesson

[[TGB]] and [[NAK]] both broke out the same day. Kristjan traded NAK. Kerisdale (a short-seller research firm) put out a hit piece on NAK the same day, killing the trade. **Hit pieces are a setup risk** — they can end a breakout instantly even when the chart is clean.

## AMD — The Shoulda-Coulda-Woulda

> "AMD shoulda coulda woulda opening range highs were in the middle like 11.35% area, could have risked like forty-forty 5¢ to make $3, not bad, big big move."

Textbook setup, textbook entry, textbook stop, missed it. The recurring theme of these reviews.

## High-Tide Flag Entry (EXCL Example)

> "Dropped the ball on it, just great looking high tide flag, narrow range day before the breakout day and then boom. Looked like this on the sixty minute chart. So you buy it over this candle, use this candle lows as your stop and just let it ride."

**Mechanics:**
- Identify narrow range day (tight consolidation)
- Wait for breakout candle on the 60-min
- Buy above the breakout candle high
- Stop at the breakout candle low
- Let it ride

## Earnings Capper Criteria

> "When I buy earnings I want something that's very strong like a big beat and big growth, but most stocks they really don't have much growth."

He passes on ~95% of earnings cappers — only the big beat + big growth combination interests him.

## Lessons

1. **Five-star setup = tight range + volume breakout + higher lows + clean catalyst.** Memorize this anatomy.
2. **Hit pieces are an unpriceable risk.** A clean chart can die on a short-seller report the same day.
3. **High-tide flag entry** is mechanical: narrow range day → breakout → buy over candle, stop under candle.
4. **AMD/EXCL/SINA/DDD** — all five-star setups he didn't take. The pass rate on five-star setups is the real edge loss.
5. **Earnings cappers**: only trade big beat + big growth. Skip the snooze-fest beats.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. The **high-tide flag** is a high-probability entry: narrow range → breakout on volume → buy over candle, stop under candle.
2. **Five-star setups** are identified by tightness before breakout + clean catalyst — but identification ≠ execution.
3. **Sector breakouts** (steel on Feb 9, Chinese internet on Feb 6) carry multiple names simultaneously — pick the strongest.
4. **Earnings cappers** are graded on growth quality, not beat size — pass on 95%.
5. **Hit pieces** are a non-chart risk that can invalidate a perfect setup overnight.
