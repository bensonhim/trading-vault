# How to Trade (and Not to Trade) Insanity Stocks

Kristjan's definitive guide to trading microcap insanity stocks — the rare, extreme squeezes that go up thousands of percent in days. The lesson is simple: **never short early, wait for the exhaustion move, and always have a set risk-reward.**

---

## What Are Insanity Stocks?

Microcap / low-float stocks that go up **1,000%+ in a few days or weeks**. They are always microcaps. They happen a few times per year. History repeats — the same pattern every time.

> "Micro cap stocks, they can always go up thousands of percent in a few days. Always."

> "The higher they go, the faster they start moving."

## Core Rules for Insanity Stocks

1. **Never short on day 1** — wait for the exhaustion move.
2. **Never short anything up "only" 100%** — these can go to 2,000%+.
3. **Wait for thousands of percent move** before considering a short.
4. **Short the backside only** — after initial crack, bounce into resistance, or sideways consolidation.
5. **First red candle taking out previous candle's lows** = the entry trigger.
6. **Always have a set risk-reward** — use the highs as your stop, accept slippage.
7. **Never add, add, add into strength** — this is how 6-figure losses happen.
8. **Don't let one trade's emotional damage prevent the next trade** — Kristjan couldn't flip from short to long after the $52K loss, but he *could* wait patiently for the re-short.
9. **Patience is the edge** — "I refused to be early."

> "Always have that in mind if you trade them on the short side. It's easy to wait for those rare opportunities that happen a few times per year where they go up and then you short them, then you have a high probability short."

> "You always have to respect price and you have to know history. You have to know what stocks are capable of. Even the most stupid scenario can turn into something crazy."

## Key Takeaways

1. **Insanity stocks can always go further than anyone anticipates** — that's why the squeeze happens.
2. **Never short early** — wait for the exhaustion move (thousands of percent, straight up, first red candle).
3. **The entry is the first red candle taking out the previous candle's lows** — use the highs as your stop.
4. **Adding into strength = how you blow up** — AQXP cost $86K, KBIO cost 25% of equity.
5. **Patience + pattern knowledge = the edge** — Kristjan studied prior insanity stocks intraday to know exactly what to look for on BPTH.
6. **Emotional control after a loss is critical** — couldn't flip long after $52K loss, but could wait for the re-short.
7. **Box shares on hard-to-borrow stocks** if you want to hold through volatility.
8. **Never hold microcap shorts over the weekend.**
9. **The real money is on the long side** after 20% market declines — Kristjan is primarily a swing long trader.
10. **Follow price, not opinions** — "even the most stupid scenario can turn into something crazy."
