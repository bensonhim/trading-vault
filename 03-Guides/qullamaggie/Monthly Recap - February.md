Kristjan made **$101K** in February with a **43% win rate** — a decent month, but he estimates he could have made 50–100% more by eliminating his top mistakes: premature exits on earnings trades, under-sizing five-star shorts, and missed longs.

## Mistake Category 1: Not Following Exit Rules on Earnings Trades

Kristjan kept getting stopped out on support, then stocks ripped the next day.

| Stock | Entry | Stop Used | Should Have Used | Result |
|-------|-------|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Pixiworx | Bought breakout | Too tight | Breakeven | Stopped out → spiked 20%+ next day |

| [[GOGO]] | Bought | Too tight | Wider stop | Stopped out → straight up |

- Missed a 5-figure trade

> "I'm usually in front of the computer an hour and a half before the market opens but not on this day. Very annoying, very avoidable."

## Key Takeaways

1. **Follow your exit rules on earnings trades** — use wider stops or breakeven, not tight stops on support.
2. **Size in on five-star shorts** — when the setup is perfect (day 3–4, parabolic, range break), risk 2–3x your normal amount.
3. **Be patient on covers** — these stocks can crash 30–50% in a day; don't cover in the first 10% of the move.
4. **Box all shares** on hard-to-borrow shorts if you want to hold through a squeeze.
5. **Don't let boredom kill slow-moving longs** — use breakeven stops and let them work.
6. **Be at your computer pre-market** — missing a 5-figure trade because you weren't there is the most avoidable mistake.
7. **Mental exhaustion from one trade can cost you on the next** — the CMBX squeeze drained his ability to size in on a concurrent setup.
8. **43% win rate is normal** — profitability comes from position sizing on the best setups, not from win rate.
