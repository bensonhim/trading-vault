## The 3 Timeless Setups

### 1. Breakouts

Stocks move in stair-steps: big move → orderly pullback → range expansion (breakout).

**3 Steps:**
1. Big move higher (30-100%+) in past 1-3 months
2. Orderly pullback/consolidation (2 weeks - 2 months) with higher lows, tightening range, surfing 10/20/50-day MA
3. Range expansion (breakout) out of consolidation

**Entry:** Opening range highs (1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle)
**Stop:** Lows of the day, not wider than ATR/ADR
**Exit:** Sell 1/3 to 1/2 after 3-5 days, move stop to breakeven, trail with 10/20-day MA
**R/R:** 10-20x+ initial risk in bull market

**How to find:** Scan for top 1-2% stocks up most over 1-month, 3-month, 6-month timeframes

→ [[swing-trading-school/ZdXxuBIFTZ0_Timeforpatience-SwingTradingSchoolfirsthourorso|Swing Trading School: Patience and Breakouts]]

### 2. Episodic Pivot (EP)

Unexpected good news triggers multi-month, multi-year moves.

**4 Conditions:**
1. Gap up 10%+
2. Big volume — average daily volume in first 15-30 minutes
3. Big growth numbers (preferably triple-digit EPS/revenue growth + significant beat)
4. Best if stock hasn't rallied in past 3-6 months (genuine surprise)

**Entry:** Opening range highs
**Stop:** Lows of the day
**Exit:** Trail with 10/20-day MA
**Mastery time:** 4-8 earnings seasons (1-2 years)

**Inherited from Pradeep Bonde's EP 9M setup.** Key difference: Kristjan uses 10%+ gap threshold (vs Pradeep's 4%+) and measures volume by "average daily volume in first 15-30 min" (vs Pradeep's 9M total daily volume).

→ [[swing-trading-school/2f1nX_TGelQ_Earningsseason-findingthenextbigwinners-Swingtradi|Swing Trading School: Earnings Season]]
→ [[How to Trade Insanity Stocks|How to Trade Insanity Stocks]]

### 3. Parabolic Short (or Long)

Stocks are like rubber bands — when stretched, they snap back.

**2 Conditions:**
1. Stock up 50-100%+ (large cap) or 300-1000%+ (small cap) in days/weeks
2. Up 3-5+ days in a row

**Entry (short):** Opening range lows, first red 5-min candle, or VWAP fail
**Stop:** Highs of the day or VWAP reclaim
**Target:** 10/20-day moving averages
**R/R:** 5-10x (lower than other setups but higher win rate)

**Key:** Don't be too early. Let amateur shorts get run over first — that's where your edge comes from.

## Scanning Process

### Nightly Scans (TC2000)

| Scan | Purpose | Key Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| ADR stocks | Foreign ADRs | Price ≥$1, Vol ≥40K, $Vol ≥$1.5M, ADR ≥2.2% |
| Biggest 1-month gainers | Momentum longs/shorts | Top 3% by price growth, high $vol, high ADR |
| Trend Intensity | Pure high-trend scan | TI ≥108 + liquidity + ADR ≥2.2% |
| 5-day gainers | Best short ideas | Stocks strongest past 5 days |
| 5-day losers | Mean reversion longs | Stocks weakest past 5 days |
| Biggest 1yr/2yr losers | Beaten-down bounce plays | Bottom 3.45% over 1-2 years |
| OTC scan | OTC stocks | Lower price, higher ADR |
| Recent IPOs | IPO momentum | Price growth 1yr = blank (under 1yr old) |

### Intraday Scans

| Scan | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Today's momentum watchlist | Stocks up ≥55% of ADR intraday |
| Stocks up ≥7% | News-driven breakouts |
| ADR intraday | Which ADRs are up today |
| OTC intraday | What's moving on OTC |

### Key Filter: ADR ≥ 2.2%

> "I just like it to weed the slower moving stocks. I want stocks that have range."

ADR (Average Daily Range) = intraday range only, excludes gaps. Measured over 20 days. Cutoff at 2.2% filters out slow-moving stocks.

### Trend Intensity (Inherited from Pradeep)

- TI = how much 13-day MA is above/below 65-day MA
- TI = 90 → faster MA no more than 10% below slower MA
- TI = 110 → faster MA at least 10% above slower MA
- Kristjan scans for TI ≥108 for pure momentum

## Key Lessons (From 72 Curated Notes)

### 1. Don't Anticipate — Wait for Confirmation
> "There was really no reason to short it before the stock actually broke this trend line, but I tried to anticipate it... just waste of time and effort and mental energy."

### 2. Too-Tight Stops Can Be Worse Than Too-Loose
> "Sometimes too tight stops lead you to miss out the real move."

### 3. Size In on Microcap Range Breaks
Three microcap runners where Kristjan had the right idea but failed to add at the breakdown — left significant profit on the table.

### 4. 25-30% Win Rate Is Profitable
> "You can be wildly profitable with having just a 25-30% win rate. It's all about having small losses and big winners."

### 5. Let Amateurs Get Run Over First (Parabolic Short)
> "Don't be too early. WAIT for the right setup. Let the amateur shorts get run over, that's where your edge comes in."

### 6. Evernote Database — 1000+ Hours
> "I have an Evernote database I worked on for 7-8 years where I track these setups. I have probably spent 1000+ hours building it."

### 7. Setup Selection Over Market Timing
Focus on finding the right setups rather than predicting market direction. The setups work across all market conditions — they just occur more frequently in certain regimes.

### 8. Earnings Season = Best Hunting
Earnings and earnings guidance are the primary EP catalysts. 4-8 earnings seasons needed to master the EP setup.

### 9. VWAP Is the Only Intraday Indicator
> "VWAP is the only intraday indicator I really use."

### 10. Sell Rules
- Sell 1/3 to 1/2 after 3-5 days
- Move stop to breakeven on remaining position
- Trail with 10-day MA (fast stocks) or 20-day MA (slower stocks)
- Wait for first CLOSE below the MA
