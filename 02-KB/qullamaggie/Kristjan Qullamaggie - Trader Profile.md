## 3 Timeless Setups

### 1. Breakouts
- Stock makes 30-100%+ move, pulls back orderly (2 weeks - 2 months), then breaks out
- Scan for top 1-2% stocks up most over 1-month, 3-month, 6-month timeframes
- Enter on opening range highs (1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle)
- Stop: lows of the day, not wider than ATR/ADR
- Sell 1/3 to 1/2 after 3-5 days, move stop to breakeven, trail with 10/20-day MA
- In bull market: 10-20x+ initial risk possible

### 2. Episodic Pivot (EP)
- Gap up 10%+ on unexpected good news (earnings, FDA, government)
- Big volume — average daily volume in first 15-30 minutes
- Best if stock hasn't rallied in past 3-6 months (genuine surprise)
- Enter on opening range highs, stop at lows of the day
- Trail with 10/20-day MA
- Takes 4-8 earnings seasons (1-2 years) to master

### 3. Parabolic Short (or Long)
- Stock up 50-100%+ (large cap) or 300-1000%+ (small cap) in days/weeks
- Up 3-5+ days in a row
- Short on opening range lows or first red 5-min candle
- Target: 10/20-day moving averages
- Wait for the right setup — let amateur shorts get run over first
- 5-10x risk reward (lower than other setups but higher win rate)
