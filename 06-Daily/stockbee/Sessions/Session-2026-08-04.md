# Three-Day Bounce, FOMO in Full Fridge, and CLF as the Clean One

August 4 was the third day of a market bounce — the best breadth day in a while (500+), but Pradeep remained cautious. The AM was dominated by big-cap earnings reactions: Microsoft already 24%, Palantir 30%+, Amazon gapping up. The PM session saw FOMO "in full fridge" — phone ringing, emails, WhatsApp messages. Pradeep's stance: not even 5% invested, waiting for clean setups, not chasing. CLF was the one clean breakout with a clear catalyst (government steel tariff, turnaround from $154→$147→$130 losses). AXDX was the only EP 9M candidate. SMCI was the cleanest of the messy setups. The session featured Leopold's comprehensive destruction, the GMV genius who sold to fools, and the 17th century wife-selling history revisited.
