# 20% Study at 292 — Highest in History, Consolidation Coming, and the AXTX Trade

August 5 was a day of process teaching and market observation. The 20% study hit 292 — the highest reading in history, even surpassing COVID. But Pradeep explained that at the beginning of a move after a correction, this reading isn't as problematic as it becomes on the second or third leg. The expectation: sideways consolidation that creates low-risk entry points. The key scan introduced was ADS 15 (stocks up 15% in 5 days) — this is where you find the stocks that made big 4-day moves and are now setting up for second legs. AXTX was the one trade Pradeep took — entered at open, stopped out for 20% profit in 30 minutes. The PM session was mostly empty — 936 stocks in combo but nothing actionable, continuation setups too early (rally only 3-4 days old), shorts not compelling. The session featured the "37% guide completion" stat, the suicide rescue operations, and the observation that no woman has ever blown up and contacted Pradeep.
