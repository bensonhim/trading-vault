# Sideways Chop with Bearish Bias — CapEx Is Poison and Earnings Rejection Season

July 27 was a Monday with only an AM meeting (no PM). Pradeep's SA was clear: sideways chop with a bearish bias, no big 20% moves, breakouts unlikely to follow through. The key narrative shift: AI earnings are now being **rejected** — even ASML, Taiwan Semi, and Google had good earnings but the market sold them off. The new keyword is **CapEx is poison** — funds are worried about the massive AI infrastructure spending. The best performing DEPs come from only three sectors: technology, healthcare, and consumer discretionary. Banks are out. The session ended early — Pradeep was recovering from a boot camp weekend with 20 one-on-one meetings and needed to rest his voice.

## AM Meeting (9:15 AM)

### SA: Sideways Chop, Bearish Bias

> "No big 20% moves happening, no big 25% to the downside. We're just sideways chop with a bearish bias."

> "These numbers are also like an API — lot of selling coming in. Probability of a bounce would have been much higher, but we're in a dead zone of selling, not much buying."

### The AI Narrative Has Changed

> "The narrative for AI was very bullish. The suppliers to AI — memory, photonics, everything — were going up like crazy. Now it has all gone into a sideways action."

> "Market thinks these people are just wasting their money putting so much into AI."

### CapEx Is Poison

> "Earlier, the keyword was 'bottleneck, bottleneck.' Now, to open the bottleneck, they're putting tons of CapEx, and CapEx has become a naked keyword. CapEx is poison now."

### Earnings Rejection Season

> "Last earnings season, earnings was being rewarded. This earnings season, if you have good earnings, the market expected you to have them."

> "ASML had good earnings — rejected. Taiwan Semi had very good earnings — rejected. Google earnings were not bad — sold off because of AI investment concerns."

> "It's like telling someone: 'You can do salsa dancing? We expected you to do salsa dancing. Show us — can you do ballroom dancing?'"

### What to Do: SIPs or Wait

> "All you have to do is look for a setup like SIPs or something where there is a reason or a catalyst. For the down move is where I would focus on at this stage."

> "Control the controllable. External environment is not under your control. What is under control is your setups, your risk management, where you put stops, where you take profits."

### DEP Sectors: Only Three

> "The best performing DEPs will come from three sectors: technology, healthcare, and consumer discretionary. The probability of them coming from a bank is equal to..."

> "Only time I would get excited about a bank is if there's a recession and 18 months bearish market and all banks beaten down to single digits. Then if JPMorgan is at $5 or Bank of America at $2, I'd probably get excited."

## Key Takeaways

1. **Sideways chop with bearish bias** — No big 20% moves, breakouts unlikely to follow through, selling pressure visible in breadth.
2. **AI narrative has changed** — Funds are worried about massive CapEx spending. "CapEx is poison" is the new keyword replacing "bottleneck."
3. **Earnings rejection season** — ASML, Taiwan Semi, Google all had good earnings and were sold off. Market expected good earnings; now wants to see more.
4. **DEPs only from 3 sectors** — Technology, healthcare, consumer discretionary. Banks will never produce good DEPs (unless in a recession at single-digit prices).
5. **Shorts working better than longs** — Both sides low, but shorts have more follow-through on the downside.
6. **Don't short on Day 2** — Golden rule: if you miss the first day, don't chase. Only trade the first date.
7. **SIPs or wait** — Focus on stocks with a fresh catalyst for the day, or wait for better conditions.
8. **Control the controllable** — External environment is not under your control. Setups, risk management, stops, profit-taking are.
9. **Self-leadership is #1** — The most important skill for a trader. Confidence comes from process.
10. **No secret to trading** — Everything Pradeep teaches is on the site. There's no hidden knowledge he shares only in one-on-ones.
11. **Elon Musk was smart** — Sold at the right time to the gullible public. ChatGPT and Anthropic IPOs will go down from day one.
12. **Recovering from boot camp** — 20 one-on-one meetings, can't eat or pee. Next boot camp: Saturday 8 AM to 8 PM instead of Friday.
