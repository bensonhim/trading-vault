# Winding Down — EP 9M and DEP Only, Semis in Doldrums, and the Victoria's Secret Standard

August 3 was a quiet Monday after the Leopold blow-up bounce. Pradeep's SA was clear: range-bound chop continues, AI trade imploded, semis in doldrums ("do not touch"), only EP 9M and DEP with catalyst are worth trading. The AM session was dominated by big-cap tech earnings reactions — Microsoft, Amazon, and Google all gapped up but the question is how much upside is left on slow-moving stocks. The PM session was short — 936 stocks in combination bullish but almost nothing actionable. Pradeep introduced the "Victoria's Secret Standard" (only trade A+ setups, not Walmart/Dollar Tree setups), discussed his winding-down phase of life (Medicare, stem therapy, lower risk appetite), and shared stories about Market Wizards' early crazy risk-taking vs their conservative advice now.

---

## AM Meeting (9:15 AM)

### SA: Range-Bound Chop, AI Drag Continues

> "It has been a choppy thing. We had this binge of buying, V-shaped recovery, AI stocks went up 300-400% in two months. Since then the AI trade has imploded."

> "Before the hedge fund blowing up information came to us, it was already clear that the big funds were getting out. How? If you look at most of those stocks, they had series of EP 9M to the downside. 3-4-5-6-7-8-9 EP 9M in a row within a month = big boys getting out."

### 20% Study: Not Jumping

> "20% study is really not jumping. At the bottom it was 800+ within 3-4 days. Now there's nothing much in terms of buying/selling. We'll remain in a range-bound choppy action."

### SOXL as Leading Indicator

> "Every time the market bounces back, which triple ETF does the best? SOXL. And SOXL is just struggling. That's the leading indicator."

### Breakouts Selective, Catalyst Required

> "Pattern-based trades have a lower probability of working. Focus on something with a specific catalyst related to that stock."

> "If the answer is resoundingly yes [breakouts likely to work], go buy breakouts. If maybe, you're careful. If no, ask: are breakdowns likely to work?"

### Tesla = Cult Stock, Avoid

> "Tesla is a cult stock. They move based on cult following and the cult leader making a tweet. Focus on easier stocks to trade than a cult stock."

> "There will be a clear setup, EP 9M. Next day, some news, you get stopped out. You need a real catalyst — missed earnings or massive negative catalyst."

### Microsoft and Amazon — Delayed Reaction EP

> "Microsoft made almost 24% move. I was expecting 15-20% after earnings. It's a slow-moving stock — glacial. How much more is left?"

> "Amazon thought it would set up as a delayed reaction EP. It just went crazy."

> "Microsoft — I would expect 40-50% move if it makes a move. But the sector is in complete doldrums."

### Semiconductors: Do Not Touch

> "Semiconductor stocks — there is just no go zone currently. From a darling of the market, it has become almost don't touch."

### Winding Down — Personal Phase

> "As I wind down my life work, the only setup I'm interested in is EP and EP 9M."

> "I'm 63. My mailbox is full of Medicare consultants, Roth conversion consultants, long-term care offers."

> "That's why my risk-taking ability is lower than 25 years ago. Do I really want to take crazy risk here? No. Not going to take any crazy risk. That phase of life is over."

> "Mark Minervini told us at dinner: he put 150-170% on a restaurant stock. Made his first million. Now his book says: don't have more than 12.5% in one position."

> "Lance Bernstein and others — trading other people's money. Zero skin in the game."
