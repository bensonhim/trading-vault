# Don't Short on Day Three — Exhaustion Setups, Trapped Buyers, and the Okra Selection Method

July 28 was a day of execution and teaching. Pradeep made two successful SIPs trades — Palantir short (entered at open, 80% closed for profit in minutes) and NVDA short (entered after gym). Both used the "trapped buyers" concept: yesterday's breakout buyers are today's trapped sellers. The AM session was a deep dive into the 20% study as absolute momentum, the secular bull market framework, and the "London Calling" origin story (June 4-5 breakout failures as the signal). The PM session found GLW as the best exhaustion/reversal bullish setup, TQQQ as the market-level reversal play, and featured the memorable "okra selection" story about not overthinking things. Key lesson: don't short on Day 3 of a downtrend — Friday was the day to short, today the risk/reward is gone.

## AM Meeting (9:15 AM)

### SA: Dead Zone, Slow Boiling Frog

> "20% study is almost in a dead zone. Not much buying, not much 20% moves. Stocks are not making big moves to the downside either. Slow boiling of the frog kind of action."

> "Before this dead zone, you had one of the best rallies in many years. 100+ days of stocks making 20%+ moves, reaching as high as 232. That was the highest reading bar none in many years — even higher than COVID."

### Secular Bull Market Framework

> "We are in a secular bull market that started in 2009. In 2023, ChatGPT was launched, giving further fuel. Secular bull markets last 20-25 years and end with speculative bubbles, leverage, inflation, and aggressive rate hikes."

> "How do you know there's a speculative bubble? When you get 1,000+ IPOs in a year. When every company adds AI. The day Mark Zuckerberg changes Meta's name to something AI is the day you know."

### London Calling Origin (Revisited)

> "On June 4 or 5, many semiconductor breakouts failed. They opened with a gap and started selling off. I had about $250K in open profit that evaporated in minutes. I closed for small losses."

> "After that, I made the London Calling video saying we'd be in a sideways market for quite some time. That's how it played out."

### The 20% Study Is Absolute Momentum

> "20% study is a study of absolute momentum. It's not relative. Relatively, banks are doing well. Relatively, Patel Motel REITs are doing well. But do you want to go there?"

> "If stocks are performing well, why would the 20% study be below 20? If they're making 20%+ moves, the 20% study would be going up."

### What Works: SIPs, Selective Shorts, Selective Breakouts

> "SIPs for intraday day trading, select breakouts, EP 9M, some select DEP and reversals."

> "Death by thousand cuts is likely here. A lot of people are down 40% on positions, telling each other 'this is all part of rotation.'"

### PLTR Short — Trapped Buyers SIPs

> "Palantir — keep an eye on it. As a short, it might work very well because yesterday it had a breakout. That kind of thing traps a lot of people."

> "I started Palantir at the open. I already closed 80% position. That's a SIPs. Free money."

> "Entry: 125.83. OPG. If you have a setup, run EP 9M scan, see it gapping down 4%, you know there are going to be trapped buyers."

### NVDA Short — After Gym

> "NVDA — when I came back from the gym, I shorted it at around 135."

> "Same logic. Yesterday people were saying Bitcoin is back, software is rotating. Where do you think they'll be today?"

### Don't Short on Day Three

> "Three days in a row, the market is down. Don't short on the third day. Friday was the time to put shorts."

> "If I was not conducting the boot camp, I can guarantee you 100% of my account would have been short on Friday. SNDK, MU, all of those stocks were showing EP 9M to the downside on Friday."

> "Don't kick a dog which is already half dead."

### 6% Drawdown Angle Point

> "6% is my angle point. If I get below 6%, I stop trading and reevaluate everything. Getting out of drawdown is the worst thing in life."

> "The whole idea behind situational awareness is to not get into large drawdowns. Not to get into them in the first place."

> "If you want to play reversal bullish, play the overall market as a reversal bullish setup rather than trade individual stocks. TQQQ or SOXL."

### Exhaustion Setup Explained

> "Exhaustion is something which happens after you tried to run a marathon. Sellers have exhausted themselves, buyers step in."

> "Good exhaustion setups happen below 50-day moving average, below 200-day moving average. By definition, exhaustion is selling exhaustion. Sellers exhausted, buying comes in."

> "You're trading for 3-4 days. Not long-term holding."

> "Most reversal bullish setups that will work have every reason why you should NOT get involved. Below 200-day moving average. Every reason not to buy will be there."

### 10 Good Exhaustion Trades Per Year

> "In a year, you're only going to get 10 good exhaustion setups which will be the way APP worked. That's what you're looking for."

## Key Takeaways

2. **Secular bull market intact** — Started 2009, fueled by ChatGPT in 2023. Ends with bubble (1000+ IPOs), leverage, inflation, rate hikes. Not there yet.
3. **London Calling validated** — June 4-5 breakout failures in semis were the signal. 20% study at 232 (highest in years) = unsustainable momentum. Pradeep called it.
4. **Don't short on Day 3** — Friday was the day to short (SNDK, MU, AMD all showed EP 9M downside). Day 3 = late, risk/reward gone, counter-trend bounce likely.
5. **Trapped buyers = best short setup** — Yesterday's breakout buyers become today's trapped sellers. PLTR and NVDA both worked as SIPs shorts using this logic.
6. **PLTR short = free money** — Entered at open (125.83 OPG), closed 80% in minutes. Procedural memory: gap down + trapped buyers = short.
7. **6% drawdown is the angle point** — If below 6%, stop trading and reevaluate. Avoiding large drawdowns is the whole point of SA.
8. **Earnings rejection season continues** — ASML, TSM, GOOG all had good earnings, all rejected. Market expected good earnings; CapEx is poison.
9. **GLW = best exhaustion setup** — Perfect reversal bullish. Selling exhausted. Stop ~$125. 10 good exhaustion trades per year — this might be one.
10. **TQQQ/SOXL for market-level reversal** — Better than individual stocks for reversal bullish. 100+ tech stocks showing exhaustion.
11. **Bounce likely tomorrow** — 100+ tech stocks showing exhaustion setups. Gap-up bounce probable.
12. **Okra selection method** — Don't overthink. Pick a bunch, some will be bad. Bigger fish to fry than 40 minutes on okras.
13. **EP 9M near all-time high = short signal** — Pradeep's rule: EP 9M near ATH is when the market seriously starts going down.
14. **Relative strength works in bull markets** — "Everything works when the market is going up. Try kissing your stepmom after the market turns and you'll be an orphan."
